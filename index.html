How it works

Four steps. Then it runs on its own.

No new habits. You keep using Tally and WhatsApp exactly as you do today.

Step On their phone On your side

  1. 01Read

    Nothing. Your customers install nothing.

    ASVA sits next to TallyPrime and reads what is outstanding. It writes back one thing: a receipt, after you confirm.

  2. 02Decide

    Still nothing. No one is messaged at night.

    It picks who to nudge today from each party’s credit days, and writes the message in their language.

  3. 03Send

    The real Tally bill and a polite reminder, with a pay link that opens UPI.

    From your shop’s own WhatsApp number. One message per customer, daytime only.

  4. 04Reconcile

    They answer as they always do: “paid”, a screenshot, a voice note, a date.

    A promise or a dispute pauses that customer. “Paid” reaches you the same minute. One tap posts the receipt.

What ASVA does

It reads “kal bhej dunga” the way you would.

English, Hinglish, Hindi or Tamil. Typed, a screenshot or a voice note. If a reply is unclear, it asks you. It never answers your customers on its own.
