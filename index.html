Questions

Straight answers before you download.

What shop owners and their accountants ask us most.

What is the best WhatsApp payment reminder software for TallyPrime?

It depends on the job. For chasing overdue credit customers automatically from your own WhatsApp number, with replies understood and receipts posted back into Tally, ASVA is built specifically for that. For credit limits and buyer risk scoring, Kenso is stronger. For a broad accounting suite, look at CredFlow or Takkada. ASVA costs Rs 699 to Rs 1,499 a month by number of active parties, with no per-message charges.

Does ASVA send reminders from my own WhatsApp number?

Yes. Bills and reminders go out from the shop's own WhatsApp number, so customers recognise the sender. Sending is paced and capped per hour, daytime only, with one combined message per customer.

Does ASVA change my Tally data?

It reads your bills and balances. The only thing it writes is a receipt voucher, and only after you confirm the amount. It never edits or deletes existing vouchers and never creates sales vouchers.

How much does ASVA cost?

14 days free from the day you start, then Lite Rs 699 a month for up to 150 active parties, Collect Rs 999 for up to 400, and Recover Rs 1,499 for up to 1,000 with AI reply handling and multi-company. Above 1,000 is priced on request. No setup fee, no per-message charges.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They get a normal WhatsApp message from the shop's number with a pay link that opens in any browser and hands off to any UPI app.

Which languages does ASVA use?

Reminders go in English by default, or Hinglish for the customers you choose. ASVA understands replies in English, Hinglish, Hindi and Tamil.

How do I get ASVA set up?

Download the Windows installer on the computer running TallyPrime, enter the short setup code, pick your Tally company and scan WhatsApp once. It takes about five minutes, and you can message +91 93441 10272 on WhatsApp to be walked through it.
