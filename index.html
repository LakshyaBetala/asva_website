How it works

Four steps. Then it runs on its own.

No new habits. You keep using Tally and WhatsApp exactly as you do today.

Step 01 · Read

Tally is the truth

ASVA sits next to Tally and reads what's outstanding. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is edited.

Step 02 · Decide

ASVA composes

It picks who to nudge today by each party's credit terms, then drafts a message in their language.

Step 03 · Send

Your own WhatsApp

The bill and reminder go out from your shop's number, with a UPI pay link. Customers install nothing.

Step 04 · Reconcile

Payment posts back

A "paid" reply pauses reminders. You confirm the amount and post the receipt into Tally in one tap.

What's new

Shipping improvements, version after version.

The last few updates, newest first. Your app updates itself, so you always have the latest.

v1.9.0 · latestSee your cash coming in, know who pays on time

A weekly Cash Coming In view shows how much is likely to arrive. A monthly Recovery Report Card shows how much came back versus last month. Every customer gets a reliability grade built from real data. Ask on WhatsApp with CASH, CARD or SCORE. WhatsApp is steadier, and duplicate phone numbers no longer trip up the Payments tab.

v1.8.10Shop WhatsApp stays quiet with customers

Your shop's own WhatsApp number never auto-replies to a customer. It quietly reads paid confirmations and payment screenshots, matches them to the right party, and drops them into your Payments tab, telling you privately. Customers only ever hear from you.

v1.8.9Steadier WhatsApp, payments capture themselves

WhatsApp rides out brief hiccups without asking for a new QR scan. Customer "paid" messages and UPI screenshots are read automatically, amounts matched, and dropped into Payments ready to post into Tally.

v1.8.8New customers sync on their own

Add a customer in Tally and raise their first bill, ASVA picks them up on the next refresh with the bill. It reads the customer's number in the expected order: WhatsApp field, then Primary Mobile, then address.

v1.8.7Smoother payments, clearer chat, a guided tour

Recording a payment into Tally is rock solid, with no double entries. Same-name parties show a numbered list. The phone app is redesigned with a Chase-today list and one-tap calling. A skippable guided tour walks new owners through every feature.

v1.8.6Enter a payment into Tally in one tap

Customer "paid" replies show up in a new Payments tab. Confirm the amount, the deposit account and the bill it clears, then post the receipt straight into Tally in one tap. Reminders now always carry your bank details.

v1.8.5Speaks your language, finds the right party

English or Hinglish, in the app and on WhatsApp. Finds the right party from a short name or small typo, keeps same-name shops apart, and asks when unsure. RECOVERED shows how much came back this month.

v1.8.4Knows when a customer promises to pay

When a customer says they've paid or promises a date, ASVA pauses that party's reminders and shows you why, with the customer's own message.

v1.8.3Steadier, and updates itself

Auto-updates. Dark mode. A clearer dashboard showing party and outstanding count. A do-not-chase list. One-tap connection check that reports issues in plain words.

v1.8Promise-to-Pay

ASVA reads your customer's WhatsApp reply. If they've paid or promise a date like '5 tareek', reminders pause and you're nudged to record it in Tally.

v1.7Morning checkpoint

Before the day's reminders go out, ASVA shows you the list so you can hold anyone who already paid.

v1.6One-click installer

A single Windows installer with no keys to type. Type a short code, pick your Tally company, scan WhatsApp, live in about five minutes.
