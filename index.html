What ASVA does every day

You keep using Tally and WhatsApp exactly as you do now. ASVA works in between.

  1. Reads your Tally

    Every few minutes ASVA reads what is outstanding, party by party and bill by bill, straight from TallyPrime. Nothing to export, no add-on to install.

  2. Sends bills and reminders

    New bills reach the customer a few minutes after you make them. Reminders follow each party's credit days, one message per customer with all their bills and a pay link.

  3. Reads the reply

    "Paid", a screenshot, or "next Monday": ASVA understands it, pauses reminders where it should, and tells you. Unclear replies come to you, never guessed.

  4. Closes the loop in Tally

    Payments line up in one list. Check the amount, tap once, and the receipt goes into Tally against the oldest bills first.

Questions shop owners ask

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They get a normal WhatsApp message from your shop's number. The pay link opens in their phone's browser and hands off to any UPI app.

Will WhatsApp block my number?

ASVA sends the way a careful person would: one message per customer that combines all their bills, a capped number per hour, and only in the daytime. No tool can promise zero risk, but these limits keep your number looking like a normal shop.

Does ASVA change my Tally data?

ASVA reads your outstanding from TallyPrime. The only thing it ever adds is a receipt voucher, and only after you confirm the amount. It never edits or deletes your vouchers.

Which languages do the reminders use?

English by default. You can switch a group of customers to Hinglish. ASVA understands replies in English, Hinglish, Hindi and Tamil.

What counts as an active party for the plans?

A customer who still owes you and has a WhatsApp number, so ASVA can actually remind them. Fully paid customers and customers without a number do not count.
