v2.2.0 · latestA calmer, simpler ASVA: five places, one language menu, English by default

Five places on the left: Today, Parties, Payments, Reminders and Settings, with WhatsApp and Reports at the bottom. One language menu at the top; ASVA and your customer messages are in English unless you pick Hinglish. Tour, Phone app, update check and dark mode sit under More. New clean paper-and-blue screens. Nothing about your data or reminders changes.

v2.1.1Clean up parties you removed from Tally, plus all the 2.1.0 fixes

If you created a party in Tally and later deleted it, open Status and press "Clean up parties removed from Tally". After a confirmation, any customer no longer in your Tally is hidden and stops getting reminders. Nothing is deleted: their history is kept, and they come back on their own if they return to Tally. Includes all the 2.1.0 fixes below.

v2.1.0Reliable payments, Tally never crashes, your work is never wiped by a sync

Recording a payment by hand now works every time (no more "could not save"). If you open Tally after ASVA, ASVA waits for Tally to finish loading before reading it, so it no longer makes Tally close with a memory error. While you tick parties for reminders or type in the search box, a background Tally sync no longer reloads the page and loses your work. Payments you received in Tally now show correctly on the customer page (the latest ones, newest first), even if the name had a small difference. And there is a new "Name A to Z (owing only)" filter to see just who owes you.

v2.0.9WhatsApp button works, version always shown, updates never get stuck

The WhatsApp button on Today now opens the chat with that customer instead of doing nothing. You can always see which version you are on at the bottom of the left menu, and if an update ever stalls a "Download manually" link appears so you are never left waiting. The installer keeps your Start-menu and Desktop shortcuts on every install and update, so searching "ASVA" always opens the real app. On a small laptop the customer list folds into clean cards so the WhatsApp and action buttons never slide off the edge, the Today and Payments screens open correctly in every language, and recording a payment saves right away, can be retried if it failed, and keeps your selection while you fill it in.

v2.0.8Always findable, cleaner tables on small screens

The installer creates your Start-menu and Desktop shortcuts on every install and update, and on a small laptop the customer list folds into clean cards so the WhatsApp and action buttons never slide off the edge.

v2.0.7Three languages, reliable payments to Tally

Choose English, Hinglish or हिंदी (Devanagari) from the top bar, and the whole app follows your choice, including the Today screen and the phone app, so it never mixes languages with itself. Recording a payment now saves it right away and posts to Tally when your PC and Tally are open; a payment that failed can be retried, and your selection no longer disappears while you fill it in.

v2.0.6Never blank while updating, findable app, search a customer for payments

Updating no longer goes blank: a clear "Updating ASVA, it will reopen in a minute, your data is safe" window now stays on screen through the whole update, so it never looks like the app was deleted. ASVA also keeps its Start-menu shortcut every time it opens, so you can always search "ASVA" and open the real app instead of an old setup file. Recording a payment now lets you type a name to find the customer instantly, and Windows shows "Almmatix" as the publisher.

v2.0.5A wait screen you can see, clearer Escalate, quiet credit health

Sending a firm formal reminder to one customer is easier to read and confirm, with a clear Send button. v2.0.3Cleaner messages, clearer setup, steadier WhatsApp
